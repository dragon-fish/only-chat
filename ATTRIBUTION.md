# Attribution

An additional term under section 7(b) of the GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 (`LICENSE`), applied to only-chat by its copyright holder.

## The attribution

only-chat displays its Appropriate Legal Notices on the About page in settings
(`/settings/about`, source `src/client/views/settings-about.vue`). That page
carries this author attribution:

> Powered by Only Chat · © dragon-fish 及 only-chat 贡献者

with "Powered by Only Chat" linking to <https://github.com/dragon-fish/only-chat>.

## The term

Any version of the Program that you convey, or that people other than you
interact with over a network, must preserve that attribution in the
Appropriate Legal Notices it displays:

- **legible**, in the same place users would look for the program's legal
  notices — an About page, a settings entry or a footer;
- **with its link** to the upstream repository;
- **unaltered** in the words "Powered by Only Chat" and in the copyright line.
  The copyright line may be translated, and further names may be added after
  it.

You may restyle it, move it to another reasonable place, and add your own
notices beside it.

## What it does not require

- No logo, no badge, nothing on every page. One line of text in the legal
  notices is the whole requirement.
- Nothing for an instance used only by you: section 2 of the AGPL leaves that
  unconditional.
- It does not replace your own source offer. If you modify the Program, section
  13 asks you to offer **your** version's source; point the page's source link
  at it. The attribution link keeps pointing upstream.

## Removing it

Holders of a commercial licence may remove the attribution — see
`COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md`. Nobody else may.
