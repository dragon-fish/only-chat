# Name and logo

The name **only-chat** (also written "Only Chat") and the only-chat logo — the
artwork in `public/logo/`, wherever it appears in the repository — are not
covered by `LICENSE`, `src/plugins/LICENSE` or `PLUGIN-EXCEPTION.md`. All rights
in them are reserved by dragon-fish.

The code licences let you fork, modify and run the code. They do not let you
present your version as only-chat. This page is what keeps those two apart.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project accurately**: "built on only-chat", "a fork of
  only-chat", "a plugin for only-chat".
- **Show the unmodified logo** when linking to, writing about, reviewing or
  teaching the project.
- **Keep the name and logo on your own deployment** — modified or not, with any
  plugins — as long as it is not a Public Service as defined in
  `PLUGIN-EXCEPTION.md`: you, your household, people you invite, your
  organisation.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the name or logo as the name or branding of a fork, a redistribution or a
  Public Service. "Powered by only-chat" in a footer is fine; "only-chat" as
  the product's name is not.
- Modify the logo, or use a name or mark confusingly similar to either.
- Suggest that the project endorses, sponsors or operates your version.

## Renaming a fork

Replace the files in `public/logo/` and the places that display the name and
logo: `index.html` (title and favicon), `src/client/components/layout/sidebar-brand.vue`
and `README.md`. Everything else may keep its identifiers — package names,
database names and code symbols are not branding.
